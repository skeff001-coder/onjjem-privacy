Privacy Policy

App: Onjjem  |  Last updated: 25 May 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Onjjem ("we", "our", or "us"). Onjjem is a photographic restoration application available on Apple devices. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected, how it is used, and your rights in relation to it. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

Onjjem is designed with your privacy in mind. We do not collect, store, or transmit:

  • Personal identification information (name, email address, phone number, etc.)
  • User accounts or login credentials
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers beyond what Apple provides anonymously for crash reporting
  • Photos or images you restore using the app

3. Photo & Face Data

When you use Onjjem to restore a photograph, the app may use on-device face detection technology to identify facial regions within the image. This is used solely to apply targeted AI restoration enhancements (such as sharpening, noise reduction, and clarity improvement) to faces within your photo.

  • What face data is collected: Onjjem detects facial regions within photos you choose to restore. No biometric identifiers, facial recognition data, or data capable of identifying a person is collected.
  • How face data is used: Face region data is used exclusively in real time to improve the quality of photo restoration. It is not used for identification, tracking, advertising, or any other purpose.
  • Third-party sharing: Face data is never shared with any third party.
  • Storage and retention: All face detection processing is performed entirely on your device. No face data is written to disk, stored in a database, or retained beyond the active processing session. All intermediate face region data is discarded from memory immediately upon completion of the restoration process.

4. Photos You Choose to Print

Onjjem offers an optional printing service that allows you to order physical prints, canvases, and other products from your restored photos. If you choose to use this service, your photo will be transmitted securely to our trusted printing partners in order to fulfil your order. Our current printing partners are:

  • Bags of Love – bagsoflove.com
  • Contrado – contrado.com

These partners receive only the image file required to produce your print and the delivery address you provide. They act as data processors on our behalf and are required to handle your data securely and only for the purpose of fulfilling your order. They do not use your image or personal details for any other purpose.

Photos submitted for printing are retained by our printing partners only for as long as necessary to produce and dispatch your order, after which they are deleted in accordance with each partner's data retention policy.

Please refer to the privacy policies of our printing partners for further information:

  • Bags of Love Privacy Policy
  • Contrado Privacy Policy

5. In-App Purchases

Onjjem offers In-App Purchases processed entirely through Apple's App Store. We do not collect or store any payment information. All billing is handled by Apple. Please refer to Apple's Privacy Policy at apple.com/legal/privacy for details.

6. Analytics & Crash Reporting

Onjjem may use Apple's built-in, anonymised crash reporting to help us identify and fix technical issues. This data does not include any personally identifiable information or photos.

7. Children's Privacy

Onjjem is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

8. Your Rights

Because Onjjem does not collect personal data (other than delivery information shared with printing partners when you choose to order a print), there is generally no personal data held by us to access, correct, or delete. If you have placed a print order and wish to request deletion of the associated data, please contact us using the details below and we will liaise with our printing partners on your behalf.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
